Data processing agreement

Version 2026-10 · Last updated 17 October 2026

This agreement is part of the Terms of use. It is made under sections 20 and 21 of POPIA between the club (the responsible party) and [Your business name] (the operator). It applies automatically when the club is registered and approved.

1. What is processed, and why

The operator stores and processes the club’s membership and financial records (member names, contributions, payments, loans, interest, penalties, attendance, bank-visit and audit records, and user email addresses) for one purpose only: to provide the [Product name] service to the club.

2. The operator will

  • process the information only on the club’s documented instructions (using the service is an instruction) and treat it as confidential;
  • apply appropriate technical and organisational safeguards against loss, damage and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections, encrypted storage, role-based access and per-club separation enforced in the database;
  • not access club records except to give support the club asks for, to keep the service secure and working, or where the law requires;
  • make sure anyone it allows to handle the information is bound to confidentiality;
  • notify the club without unreasonable delay on becoming aware of a security compromise affecting its information, with the details it has;
  • help the club answer requests from people exercising their rights (access, correction, deletion), for example by providing exports;
  • on closure of the club, return the records (a downloadable backup is available in the app at any time) and delete them within 90 days, and from backups within a further 35 days, unless the law requires otherwise.

3. Sub-processors and transfers

The operator uses Supabase (database and authentication) and Vercel (hosting) to deliver the service, and binds them to security and confidentiality obligations. These providers may store information outside South Africa. The operator will keep the club informed of material changes to its sub-processors.

4. The club will

  • make sure it is entitled to record its members’ information and has told them what is recorded and why;
  • enter only information needed to run the club, and no identity, bank account or card numbers;
  • keep user access up to date and use two-step login for administrators.

5. Audit and contact

On reasonable written request the operator will give the club the information needed to show it is meeting this agreement. Security and privacy contact: [support@yourdomain.co.za]. Information Officer: [Information Officer].
