Privacy policy

Version 2026-10 · Last updated 17 October 2026

[Product name] is a service run by [Your business name] (“we”, “us”) that helps savings clubs (stokvels) keep their records. This policy explains what personal information is handled, why, and what your rights are under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 4 of 2013 (POPIA).

1. Who is responsible for what

Your club decides what member information to record and why. For the club’s records, the club is the responsible party and we are its operator: we store and process the records on the club’s behalf and only on its instructions (see the Data Processing Agreement).

We are the responsible party for the information we need to run the service itself: the sign-in details and contact details of club administrators and users, and our billing records.

2. What we collect

InformationWhy
Email address and password (stored only as a one-way hash by our authentication provider)To let you sign in and keep the club’s records private.
Name and phone number of the person registering a clubTo approve the club and contact its administrator.
Member names, monthly contribution amounts, payments, loans, interest, penalties, attendance, bank-visit records and audit notesThese are the club’s records. They are entered by the club’s own administrators and capturers.

Subscription status and payment datesTo run our billing.

We do not collect identity numbers, bank account numbers or card numbers. Clubs must not enter them in notes. The service is for adults; we do not knowingly record information about children.

3. Who can see the information

  • Only people your club has approved, and only what their role allows. A member sees their own account; an auditor sees what the club has made available to auditors; administrators see the whole club.
  • Clubs cannot see each other’s records. This is enforced inside the database.
  • We do not read a club’s records. Our platform console shows club names and contact details, not club data. We will only access club data if the club asks us to help, or if the law requires it.

4. Where it is kept, and who else handles it

The service runs on Supabase (database and sign-in) and Vercel (website hosting). Their servers may be outside South Africa, so your information may be transferred across borders. We choose providers that apply security and data-protection commitments at least as protective as POPIA, as section 72 of POPIA requires. We do not sell personal information and do not use it for advertising.

5. How we protect it

  • Encrypted connections (HTTPS) and encrypted storage at our providers.
  • Row-level security so every user only reaches their own club’s rows.
  • Role-based access, an activity log, and optional two-step login for every user.
  • Accounts are removed from a club by its administrator at any time.

If we learn that personal information has been accessed without authority, we will tell the affected club and the Information Regulator, and where required the people affected, as soon as reasonably possible.

6. How long we keep it

For as long as the club uses the service. When a club closes its account we delete its records within 90 days, unless the law requires us to keep something. Encrypted backups roll off within a further 35 days. Billing records are kept as long as tax law requires.

7. Your rights

You may ask to see the information held about you, to have it corrected or deleted, or to object to its processing. Because the records belong to your club, the quickest route is your club’s administrator (members can already see their own record in the app). You can also write to us at [support@yourdomain.co.za] and we will pass it to the club or deal with it ourselves as the case requires, normally within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa): inforegulator.org.za, complaints.IR@inforegulator.org.za.

8. Cookies and storage

The app keeps your sign-in session and a temporary copy of your club’s data in your browser so it keeps working with a poor connection. The copy is cleared when you sign out. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.
